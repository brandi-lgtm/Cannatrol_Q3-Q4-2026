The Problem

Cannatrol is really two brands sharing one name. Among home growers, it already owns the category: forums, reviews and creators refer to it by nickname, and Cool Cure is the default comparison point against new entrants like the Vivosun VCure. Among commercial cultivators, dispensaries and executives, the opportunity is different. Cannatrol has genuine proof, including an independently tested 16% terpene-retention advantage, documented trichome preservation, and a real commercial case study at Hudson Growers Alliance, ready to be amplified with the same strength as its home-grow story.

Where Each Audience Stands

B2C, HOME GROWERAwareness
Community-proven, review-driven, category shorthand (“the ’trol”). This audience already converts on its own; it just needs a periodic push, like the August promotion.
B2B, OPERATOR & DISPENSARYAwareness
Real case studies and independent lab data already exist. This program amplifies them to build the same level of recognition Cannatrol already has with home growers.

Our Solution

mg Magazine is positioned to build on this exactly: a trade-media audience already made up of operators, dispensary buyers and executives, the precise readers Cannatrol’s existing proof was built to convince. This program points the native lead-gen placement already secured under Cannatrol’s existing contract at the Hudson case study specifically, at no additional cost, then layers three syndicated press releases, an editorially-led branded content feature and three e-newsletter placements on top, building toward MJBizCon. A separate one-month in-page leaderboard push runs alongside in August to support the B2C Cool Cure promotion, distinct from this B2B authority program.
